Fold o’Clock support

Five pocket LCD games for iPhone · Fonsica Group AB

Questions or problems? Write to adam@fonsica.se.

How do I start a game?

Hold your iPhone sideways to browse the collection and swipe to a game. Turn it upright to open that game — on iPhone Duo, half-fold it like a clamshell — and press PLAY.

What is the difference between Classic and Relaxed?

Relaxed is slower and gentler. Each mode keeps its own best score. Press MODE before you start a run to switch.

Why did my game pause?

Fold o’Clock pauses when you leave the app, when a call comes in, when you close your iPhone Duo, or when a fold or rotation moves the screen. Press RESUME and the game counts you back in for three seconds, so a fold never costs you a life.

How do I switch games?

Turn your iPhone sideways, or press the grid key, to open the collection and pick another game. A paused run ends when you open a different game; its score still counts.

What does the TIME key do?

While no game is running, TIME turns the screen into a desk clock that keeps the display awake.

I can't hear anything.

Sound follows the ring/silent switch and your volume. Check Sound in the app's settings too.

Can I play left-handed?

Yes: turn on Left-Handed Controls in the settings.

Where are my scores stored?

Only on your device. Deleting the app removes them. In the settings, press and hold RESET to clear all high scores.

Does Fold o’Clock collect data?

No. There is no account, no tracking, no ads and no network use. See the privacy policy.
