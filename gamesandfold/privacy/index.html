Games and Fold privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026 · Fonsica Group AB, Sweden

Games and Fold is a collection of five pocket LCD games for iPhone, made by Fonsica Group AB.

What we collect

Nothing. Games and Fold has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network features. The app does not ask for access to your camera, photos, location, contacts, microphone or notifications.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, haptics, left-handed controls, the last game and mode), which instructions you have seen, and your best scores. They are kept in the standard local storage on your device only and are removed when you delete the app. Fonsica Group AB never receives them.

Sharing

If you choose to share a score, the system share sheet creates an image on your device and you decide where it goes. Games and Fold does not send it anywhere itself.

Children

Games and Fold does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Fonsica Group AB · adam@fonsica.se

Svenska

Games and Fold samlar inte in några uppgifter: inget konto, ingen inloggning, ingen analys, ingen reklam, ingen spårning och ingen nätverkstrafik. Inställningar och rekord sparas bara på din enhet och försvinner när du tar bort appen. Frågor: adam@fonsica.se.
