Games and Fold privacy policy

Effective 5 November 2026 · previous version 23 October 2026 · Fonsica Group AB, Sweden

Games and Fold is a collection of pocket LCD games for iPhone, made by Fonsica Group AB.

What we collect

Nothing. Games and Fold has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not ask for access to your camera, photos, location, contacts, microphone or notifications.

Purchases

Signal Yard is free and every other game has one free run; one in-app purchase unlocks every game. Apple's App Store handles the purchase, Restore Purchases and refunds under Apple's terms, and tells the app on your device which games you own. This is the app's only network use. Fonsica Group AB does not receive your name, your payment details or your Apple Account from it.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, haptics, left-handed controls, the last game and mode), which instructions you have seen, your best scores, which free runs you have played and whether the games are unlocked. They are kept in the standard local storage on your device only and are removed when you delete the app. Fonsica Group AB never receives them.

Sharing

If you choose to share a score, the system share sheet creates an image on your device and you decide where it goes. Games and Fold does not send it anywhere itself.

Children

Games and Fold does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Svenska

Games and Fold samlar inte in några uppgifter: inget konto, ingen inloggning, ingen analys, ingen reklam och ingen spårning. Köpet som låser upp spelen sköts av App Store; Fonsica Group AB får varken ditt namn, dina betaluppgifter eller ditt Apple-konto från det. Inställningar, rekord, vilka gratisrundor du har spelat och vilka spel som är upplåsta sparas bara på din enhet och försvinner när du tar bort appen. Frågor: adam@fonsica.se.
